I am a **Postdoctoral Researcher in Machine Learning Security** at the [University of Luxembourg](https://www.uni.lu/), where I am a member of the Security, Reasoning and Validation Research Group (SerVal) at the Interdisciplinary Centre for Security, Reliability and Trust (SnT).

My research focuses on the **security and robustness of machine-learning-based systems**. I study how learning-based security mechanisms behave under adversarial manipulation, how realistic constraints shape the threat landscape, and how we can make these systems more reliable and trustworthy. Much of my work uses **malware detection** as a motivating application, while addressing broader questions in adversarial machine learning and trustworthy AI.

My interests include **adversarial machine learning**, **robustness of AI-enabled security systems**, **problem-space attacks**, and **certified robustness**.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from [Radboud University](https://www.ru.nl/), where my dissertation, *Rethinking the Security of Machine Learning in Malware Detection*, received third place in the **VERSEN PhD Thesis Award 2025**. I have also been a visiting scholar at King's College London and University College London.
